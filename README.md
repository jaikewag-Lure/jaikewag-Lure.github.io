# Lure privacy policy

**Last updated:** 24 October 2026

Lure ("the game") is made by Jaikishan Waghela ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game
and our advertising partner collect, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- There is no account and no sign-in. Your progress is stored only on your phone.
- Lure shows an ad only when you choose to watch one to earn a hint. It never shows ads between levels.
- Ads come from Unity (the LevelPlay ad mediation service and the Unity Ads network). If you allow personalised ads,
  Unity may use information from your phone to choose the ads you see. If you don't, the ads are not personalised.
- You can change your choice at any time in the game's **Settings > Personalised ads**. On an iPhone you can also
  turn tracking off in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**.

## Information that stays on your phone

The game saves your progress and preferences in a file on your phone: levels cleared, stars, best turn counts, Daily
results and streak, hints, settings, language, chosen glaze, and your answer to the ads question. This file is never
sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

## Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game does not send your progress, settings or usage to us or to any
analytics service. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

## Advertising

When the game loads or shows an ad, Unity's LevelPlay software and the Unity Ads network collect information from your
device so they can deliver ads, count and measure them, prevent fraud, and (only if you agree) personalise them. This
can include:

- device and app details, such as the phone model, operating system version, language and app version;
- identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) **only if you allow tracking**, the identifier Apple gives each
  app developer (IDFV), and identifiers created by the ad software;
- your IP address and the approximate location it indicates (country or region);
- how you interact with ads, such as views, taps, and whether you watched a rewarded ad to the end.

Unity processes this information under its own privacy policy:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

**Your choice.** The first time you open Lure, we ask whether you allow personalised ads.

- **Allow personalised ads:** we tell Unity you consent (for UK and EU privacy law) and that you have not opted out
  of the sale or sharing of personal information (for US state privacy laws). On an iPhone, Apple then asks whether
  Lure may track you, and you can still say no there.
- **Non-personalised only:** we tell Unity you do not consent and that you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal
  information. You will still see ads when you choose to watch one, but they are not chosen using your data. Apple's
  tracking question is not shown.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings. The change applies to the ads loaded after it.

## Purchases

Premium glazes are bought through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game
which items you own.

## Children

Lure is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you
believe a child has provided personal information through the game's ads, contact us and we will help you contact
Unity to have it removed.

## Your rights

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you have rights to access, correct and delete personal
information about you, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time (in the game's Settings). If you are
in the United States, you have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information: turn off
Personalised ads in Settings, or use Unity's
[Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information](https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information) page.

We hold no personal information about you on our side. For information held by Unity, contact Unity using the details
in its privacy policy, or contact us and we will help.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, mention it in the game's
update notes.
